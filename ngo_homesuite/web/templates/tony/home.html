About TONY

TONY is a comprehensive nonprofit and grant analysis toolkit designed to help organizations assess grant risk, ensure compliance, and optimize their grant portfolio.

Key Capabilities:
  • Data Ingestion: Import grants from CSV, Excel, or direct database connections
  • 4-Layer Risk Scoring: Analyzes financial health, operational sustainability, organizational capacity, and external factors
  • Compliance Evaluation: Checks against best practices and regulatory requirements
  • Predictive Calibration: Improves accuracy using your historical grant outcomes
  • Comprehensive Reporting: HTML, PDF, and JSON export formats with visualizations
  • Performance Analytics: Measure model accuracy and iterate on scoring logic
Scoring Presets:
  • Conservative: Higher risk thresholds for risk-averse organizations
  • Balanced: Standard risk assessment (recommended)
  • Lenient: Lower thresholds for flexibility-focused organizations
