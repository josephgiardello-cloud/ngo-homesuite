Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes. NGO HomeSuite uses industry-standard encryption and secure database practices to protect your data.

Q: Can I export my data?

A: Yes. You can export reports and data in various formats for analysis in other tools.

Q: Does NGO HomeSuite support multiple organizations?

A: Yes. Organizations are isolated and securely separated within the system.
